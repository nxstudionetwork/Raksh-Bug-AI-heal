🛡️ RAKSH
AI-Powered Digital Security Guardian — Protecting you from scams, phishing, fraud, and social engineering.

RAKSH (meaning "protection" in Sanskrit) is your intelligent digital guardian. It analyzes messages, links, QR codes, images, files, voice notes, and videos for scam indicators using simulated AI. Built as a frontend prototype, it demonstrates how a real AI security engine would work once connected to backend services.
